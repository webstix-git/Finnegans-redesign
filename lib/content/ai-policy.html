AI Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Our commitment

Finnegan's Wake LLC ("Finnegan's Wake," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to using artificial intelligence (AI) tools responsibly when they help us operate, improve, or support this website and our customer communications.

How we may use AI

We may use AI-assisted tools to help with website content drafts, accessibility improvements, customer-service workflows, analytics insights, and internal productivity. When AI is used, our team reviews material before it is published or used to communicate with guests.

Human oversight

Important decisions about our menu, promotions, events, and guest communications remain under human control. AI does not independently make binding decisions about your personal information or final published website content without staff review.

Data and privacy

We do not intentionally feed sensitive personal information into public AI systems for training. Information collected through our website is handled according to our Privacy Policy. Third-party AI providers may process limited operational data under their own terms when we use their services.

Accuracy and transparency

AI-generated drafts can contain errors. We strive to check facts, pricing, hours, and event details before publishing. If something on the site looks incorrect, please let us know so we can correct it quickly.
